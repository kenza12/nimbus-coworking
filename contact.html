Finding us

We are in the old print works on Rue des Ateliers, five minutes from Vieux-Port metro. Look for the black doors and the cloud above them.

  • Metro line 1, Vieux-Port, then a 5 minute walk
  • Bus 82 and 83 stop on Rue de la Republique
  • Bike racks in the courtyard, showers inside
  • Paid parking on Place des Ateliers

Or just walk in

No appointment needed on weekday mornings. Ask for anyone at the front desk, and the coffee is on the house.
